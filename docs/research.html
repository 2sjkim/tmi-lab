Research

Advancing medical imaging through AI, physics, and clinical translation.

Our approach

From imaging systems to clinical impact.

TMI Lab connects real-world imaging, reliable AI, and clinical translation to create meaningful impact in healthcare.

01

Real-world imaging systems

We study medical images as outputs of physical acquisition systems.

  • Imaging physics & geometry
  • Scanners, protocols & dose
  • Artifacts & acquisition variability
  • Real patient data
02

Clinical intelligence under image quality variation

We develop restoration and diagnostic AI that is robust in the real world.

  • Artifact reduction & restoration
  • Image quality assessment
  • Uncertainty modeling
  • Downstream task performance
03

Evidence-driven translation

We bridge AI development and real clinical impact.

  • Foundation data infrastructure
  • Task-based & reader-aligned evaluation
  • Clinical workflow integration
  • MVP, IP, and startup-driven translation
